ABANDONED HORROR SCI-FI FACILITY ASSET PACK
PACK 1 — FACILITY BASICS (FREE)

Thank you for downloading this asset pack.

This pack provides essential building blocks for creating top-down horror sci-fi environments, featuring modular structures, props, and environmental storytelling elements.

ART STYLE
• Top-down orthographic perspective
• Painterly semi-realistic rendering
• Designed for gameplay readability
• Lighting is intentionally not baked into the assets

USAGE NOTES
• Doors are designed for top-facing walls only.
• End caps should be used to terminate room boundaries.
• Additional lighting and atmosphere effects should be handled within your game engine.
• Promotional screenshots may include lighting adjustments for presentation purposes.
